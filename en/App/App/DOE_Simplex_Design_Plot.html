Introduction

The Simplex Design Plot visualizes a mixture design by plotting design points on triangular (ternary) axes. Each axis represents one mixture component, and the three component proportions sum to 1. Use this plot to see where pure blends, binary blends, interior blends, the centroid, and axial points fall in the formulation space

To Create the Simplex Design Plot

  • Activate a worksheet with your mixture design data, which is generated via Generate Mixture Design or Define Custom Design
  • Select Statistics: Quality Improvement: Design of Experiment from the Origin menu
  • Select the Analyze Design icon and choose Simplex Design Plot from the context menu

Understanding the Plot

  • Vertices: Pure mixtures, where one component is at 100%.
  • Edge midpoints/edges: Binary blends of two component.
  • Interior points: Complete blends containing all components. The centroid is the equal-proportion blend. Axial points lie along the line from the centroid toward each vertex.
