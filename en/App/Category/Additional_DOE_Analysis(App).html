2.43.4 Additional DOE Analysis

Add-DOE-Analysis


This chapter covers additional analysis tools that extend the standard design analysis, including methods for analyzing response variation, binary responses, and visualizing optimal settings across multiple responses.

Topics in this chapter:

  • Analyze Variability - Analyze the variability of the response to identify factors that reduce process variation.
  • Analyze Binary Response - Analyze a design with a binary (pass/fail) response.
  • The Overlaid Contour Plot in DOE - Overlay contour plots of multiple responses to identify factor settings that meet all response requirements.
